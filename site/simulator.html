Pressure–volume loop

The dots mark the valve events. MVC and TVC are closure of the mitral and tricuspid valves, AVO and PVO are opening of the aortic and pulmonic valves, AVC and PVC are their closure, and MVO and TVO are opening of the mitral and tricuspid valves.

The handle on the upper line changes contractility (Ees), the handle on the dashed line changes afterload (Ea), and the handle on the volume axis changes preload.
