Ventricular–arterial coupling

Pressure–volume loops for the left and right heart, generated by a physiological model. Change contractility, load and volume; watch the loop move against the normal ventricle.

Concepts

Ees, Ea, the ratio, work and efficiency, the RV. Why an in-range ratio can still be disease.

Simulator

Drag the loop. Give fluid, pressors, inotropes, pulmonary vasodilators. Replay each change step by step.

Scenarios

HFpEF, HFrEF, sepsis, compensated and decompensated PAH, acute PE, CpcPH.

Echo lab

Trace an LVOT VTI, measure TR velocity and TAPSE, mark RV volumes; check against catheter truth.

PA catheter

PVR, compliance, RC time, PAPi, single-beat Ees/Ea, and a haemodynamic calculator.

Five-minute tour

  1. Simulator, LV tab: drag the ESPVR handle down to Ees ≈ 1. Press Replay.
  2. Press Norepinephrine twice. Compare the SV change with the normal heart.
  3. Toggle Show disease with an in-range Ea/Ees.
  4. RV tab: load Acute massive PE, then PAH, compensated. Same PVR range, different RV Ees.
  5. Echo lab, decompensated PAH: measure TR velocity and TAPSE; compare TAPSE/PASP with the model Ees/Ea.
